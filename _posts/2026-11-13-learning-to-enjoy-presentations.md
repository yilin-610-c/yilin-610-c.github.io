In Spring 2026, I took DSAA3053: Introduction to Reinforcement Learning. Over the semester, I gave four presentations. Our team received the Best Project Presentation Award, dated April 2026, which was awarded to one group in the class.

I was happy about the award, but the change in how I felt about presenting means more to me. I used to be nervous about speaking in front of an audience. Having to present repeatedly gave me more opportunities to get used to it. Over those four presentations, I gradually became less afraid of standing up and explaining technical ideas to other people.

There was no single moment when the nervousness disappeared. The change was gradual, and I would not say I have become an expert speaker. What I noticed was that presenting started to feel more manageable. Confidence was something I could develop through experience, rather than something I needed to have before I began.

The award is a nice reminder of what our team did in the course. For me, it also marks a small personal step: becoming more comfortable sharing what I understand, even while I am still learning how to explain it well. Four presentations did not settle that process, but they helped me want to keep practicing.
