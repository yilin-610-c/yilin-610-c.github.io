In October 2026, I visited MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi after taking part in its Undergraduate Research Internship Program, UGRIP 2026. I had worked on smartphone-native AI agents during the summer. The internship was originally expected to be in person, but it eventually took place remotely. This invitation gave us a chance to experience the campus and meet the people we had been working with from a distance.

## Meeting beyond the screen

During the internship, I knew my supervisors and teammates through online meetings and messages. We could discuss research and keep the work moving, but our interactions were shaped by that format. Visiting MBZUAI meant finally meeting my supervisors, senior research teammates, and fellow interns in person.

One of the most meaningful parts was simply having a group meeting together. It was a familiar activity in a different setting, and that difference mattered to me. Conversations felt more spontaneous and personal. Meeting face to face gave another dimension to relationships that had developed around shared research tasks.

I had already been collaborating with these people, so this was not the beginning of working together. It was a chance to get to know the people behind the meetings and messages. The visit helped me appreciate how much the setting of a conversation can change the experience of collaboration, even when the work itself is familiar.

## Talking about research

We also participated in a research showcase and had opportunities to speak with faculty members. These exchanges gave me space to reflect on my research interests and what I might want from graduate study.

They also brought the communication side of research into focus. Working through an experiment and explaining its purpose to someone else call for different kinds of attention. Discussing ideas with researchers was a reminder to think about what a question means, why it matters, and how to make that clear. I valued having those conversations alongside the more familiar experience of discussing work within my own team.

## A few days in Abu Dhabi

The care put into the visit was apparent from my arrival. Someone met me at the airport, and even though I arrived very late at night, staff helped with transportation and accommodation. I appreciated those arrangements, especially at the end of the journey.

Beyond campus, we went to the desert for driving, performances, and stargazing, took a guided visit to Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, and visited teamLab Phenomena Abu Dhabi. I found Abu Dhabi welcoming and international, and enjoyed interacting with people from different backgrounds. These experiences were another part of getting to know the place and the people around me.

What stays with me most, though, is the chance to be with my research collaborators in person. Experiments, code, and papers are central to research, but so are the conversations and relationships that make the work possible. I am grateful to the people who arranged the visit and to my supervisors, teammates, and fellow interns for sharing it with me. After a remote summer, meeting them was an experience I was glad to have.
