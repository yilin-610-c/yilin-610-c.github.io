**Final-year project · HKUST(GZ)**

_Ongoing research; evaluation methodology under development._

How should we evaluate AI agents that develop quantization-related GPU kernels? A useful solution needs to be numerically sound and execute efficiently, which makes this a problem at the intersection of coding-agent evaluation and hardware-aware computation.

I am developing the project's scope and evaluation methodology, building on my experience with low-precision quantization. I am considering how to assess functional correctness, numerical accuracy, runtime performance, and reproducibility together, including robustness across configurations.
