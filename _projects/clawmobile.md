**MBZUAI UGRIP 2026 · June 2026–Present**

Supervisors: Prof. Chun Jason Xue and Prof. Youcheng Sun

## Research question

How can smartphone-native agents execute tasks reliably, reuse learned capabilities, and retain useful information across sessions? Real-device evaluation must account for Android automation failures, memory integration, and whether a task actually succeeded.

## My contributions

- Exploring demonstration-to-skill workflows and token-efficient execution strategies.
- Designing and prototyping a personal knowledge base for capturing and retrieving information across mobile applications, with Python and Android implementations.
- Deploying and testing cross-session memory configurations on real devices.
- Diagnosing benchmark setup, automation, memory integration, and result-verification problems to improve evaluation reliability and reproducibility.

My role focuses on prototyping, deployment, testing, and debugging within a collaborative effort. Benchmark design and the broader experimental program are team responsibilities.
