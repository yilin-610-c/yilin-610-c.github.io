**MBZUAI UGRIP 2026 · June 2026–Present · Ongoing collaboration**

Supervisors: Prof. Chun Jason Xue and Prof. Youcheng Sun

Smartphone-native agents need to carry out tasks, reuse learned capabilities, and retain useful information across sessions. Working on real devices also means understanding whether a failure comes from the agent, the automation setup, or the way success is checked.

## Prototyping agent capabilities

I explore demonstration-to-skill workflows and token-efficient execution strategies. I also design and prototype a personal knowledge base for capturing and retrieving information across mobile applications, using Python and Android implementations.

## Making evaluations reliable

Within our collaborative research, I deploy and test cross-session memory configurations on real devices. My work includes diagnosing benchmark setup, Android automation, memory integration, and result-verification problems so that evaluations are easier to reproduce and interpret.

I have continued collaborating after the summer internship. Our UGRIP 2026 project received the **Best Project Award**, one of three awarded projects among 22.
