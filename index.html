Yilin Liu

Efficient AI systems · HKUST(GZ)

I am Yilin Liu (刘奕麟), also known as Caralyn, a final-year B.Sc. student in Data Science and Big Data Technology at HKUST(GZ), expecting to graduate in June 2027. I have been on the Dean’s List for three semesters. In Fall 2025, I attended UC Berkeley through the Berkeley Global Access Program, earning a 3.914/4.000 GPA and studying computer architecture and systems programming.

My research interests center on efficient AI systems: LLM inference and serving, low-precision quantization, and GPU-aware system–algorithm co-design. I am interested in how numerical methods and hardware execution constraints jointly shape practical ML systems.

With Prof. Zeyi Wen, I study accuracy–execution trade-offs in low-bit KV-cache quantization, contributing algorithms and experiments in collaboration with GPU kernel and serving-system researchers. My ongoing final-year project explores how to evaluate AI agents on quantization kernel development tasks.

Earlier, I worked on AgenticMath, a multi-agent mathematical data synthesis project, with Prof. Jiaheng Wei. I also research reliable on-device agents and real-device evaluation with collaborators at MBZUAI, continuing work begun during UGRIP 2026.

I am preparing applications for Fall 2027 research-oriented master’s programs in Computer Science, ECE, and related areas, with a focus on ML systems and efficient AI.
